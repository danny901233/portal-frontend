DEPENDS ON PHASE 2 (ticket model). Executable spec for a coding agent.

GOAL: an email to hello@receptionmate.co.uk becomes a ticket, gets an automatic acknowledgement,
gets an AI-drafted reply for a human to approve, and staff replies thread back correctly.

--- 1. INBOUND (Mailgun) ---
Mailgun Inbound Route on hello@ POSTing to POST /api/webhooks/mailgun-inbound.
Infra Dan must do (state it in your PR description, do not attempt it): MX/route config in the
Mailgun dashboard pointing at the webhook.
New file: backend/src/routes/webhooks/mailgun-inbound.ts. Copy the shape of
routes/webhooks/meta-whatsapp.ts.
1. VERIFY the Mailgun signature (timestamp, token, signature) using MAILGUN_API_KEY (HMAC-SHA256).
   Reject 401 if it fails. Never process an unverified POST.
2. Persist the raw payload BEFORE parsing anything, so a parse bug loses nothing.
3. Return 200 fast; do the work after responding (Mailgun retries on non-200 and will duplicate).

--- 2. THREADING (this is what stops one conversation becoming five tickets) ---
In order of precedence:
a) Subject contains [RM #<number>] → append to that ticket.
b) In-Reply-To / References matches a stored outbound Message-Id → append to that ticket.
c) Otherwise → new ticket.
If the matched ticket is solved/closed → reopen (status open) and append.
Dedupe on the inbound Message-Id: if already stored, drop it (Mailgun retries).

--- 3. IDENTITY ---
Resolve sender email → SupportContact (create if new). Link userId/businessId/garageId when the
address matches a known user or garage. Unmatched senders still get a ticket, just unidentified.

--- 4. CLASSIFICATION ---
Deterministic rules FIRST, AI only for what is left:
- Known supplier/transactional domain → category 'billing', assign to Dan, exclude from support views.
- Sender is a known garage contact → category 'support'.
- Complaint language + known garage → category 'complaint', priority 'high'.
Then AI (reuse the existing OpenAI client pattern) suggests category for anything unresolved.
Store the AI's confidence in meta. Misclassification must be one click to correct in the UI.

--- 5. AUTO-ACKNOWLEDGEMENT (the only thing that sends unreviewed) ---
On NEW ticket only (never on a reply to an existing one), never for spam/bulk/no-reply senders,
and never to a sender whose address matches no-reply|noreply|donotreply|mailer-daemon|postmaster.
Send via sendEmail(). Subject: "We've got your message [RM #<number>]".

--- 6. AI DRAFT ---
Generate a suggested reply and store it as a DRAFT on the ticket (not an entry, or an entry with
kind 'note' flagged isDraft). It MUST NOT send. UI shows it above the reply box with
"Use draft" / "Discard". A human edits and sends.

--- 7. OUTBOUND ---
Replies send from MAILGUN_FROM (hello@) with:
- Subject carrying [RM #<number>].
- A stored Message-Id, and In-Reply-To/References set to the customer's last message, so it lands
  in the same email thread rather than starting a new one.
Store the sent Message-Id in SupportEntry.meta for future threading.

--- 8. ACCEPTANCE ---
1. Email to hello@ → ticket with correct contact, category, and an acknowledgement received.
2. Reply to that acknowledgement → appends to the SAME ticket, does not create a second.
3. Staff reply arrives in the customer's original email thread.
4. Replaying the same Mailgun POST twice creates exactly one ticket and one entry.
5. An unverified/forged POST is rejected 401.
6. No-reply sender → ticket created, NO acknowledgement sent.
