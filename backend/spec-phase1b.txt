DEPENDS ON PHASE 1. Executable spec for a coding agent.

GOAL: noise never reaches the queue, without ever silently losing a real customer.

--- 1. SPAM ---
Use Mailgun's spam verdict (X-Mailgun-Sflag / X-Mailgun-Sscore) plus SPF/DKIM results.
- High confidence spam → create NO ticket. Store the raw message in a quarantine table with a
  7-day retention, visible in a "Spam" view in the UI.
- NEVER auto-reply, NEVER unsubscribe, NEVER bounce. Backscatter to a spoofed sender damages our
  sending reputation.
- Start the threshold CONSERVATIVE (only clear spam). A garage's email vanishing silently is far
  worse than ten junk emails reaching the queue. Any recovery from the Spam view should log a
  counter so the threshold can be tuned on evidence.

--- 2. MARKETING / BULK ---
Detect List-Unsubscribe (and List-Unsubscribe-Post one-click). Create no ticket; surface in a
"Bulk" view with a one-click unsubscribe action that calls the one-click endpoint. Confirm the
first time for each new sender domain, then remember the decision.

--- 3. TRANSACTIONAL — NEVER UNSUBSCRIBE ---
Maintain an explicit allowlist of transactional senders: Stripe, GoCardless, Twilio, OpenAI,
LiveKit, Mailgun, Meta/Facebook, AWS, Companies House, HMRC, the bank.
These are never spam, never bulk, never unsubscribed. Category 'billing', assigned to Dan,
excluded from support views and from support metrics. Their only requirement is not getting lost.

--- 4. ACCEPTANCE ---
1. Obvious spam → no ticket, appears in Spam view, nothing sent back.
2. A marketing newsletter → no ticket, unsubscribable in one click.
3. A Stripe receipt → billing ticket assigned to Dan, never unsubscribed, not in Gab's views.
4. Recovering a false positive from Spam creates the ticket properly and increments the counter.
