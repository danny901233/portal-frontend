=== GOAL ===
One place in the portal where ALL ReceptionMate support lands — email to hello@, WhatsApp to our
ops number, portal live chat, and negative call feedback — worked as tickets with an owner and a
status. Scope is ReceptionMate only. MMH is OUT of scope.

=== HARD RULES (do not violate — these are decisions, not preferences) ===
1. hello@receptionmate.co.uk stays the ONLY support address. AI sorts billing/support/sales AFTER
   arrival. Do not create new mailboxes or aliases.
2. It lives in OUR portal. Not HighLevel, not Zendesk, not a third-party inbox.
3. The AI never sends a customer a real ANSWER unposted by a human. It DRAFTS; a human approves.
   The ONLY thing that sends automatically is the acknowledgement receipt (see rule 4).
4. Auto-acknowledgement: email YES, WhatsApp YES (only inside the 24h window), live chat NO
   (Leah has already replied), spam NEVER.
5. NEVER unsubscribe from transactional senders (Stripe, GoCardless, Twilio, OpenAI, LiveKit,
   Mailgun, Meta). Those are receipts and service notices we need.
6. NEVER auto-reply to spam. A reply to a spoofed sender damages our sending reputation.
7. Support WhatsApp = garages messaging OUR ops number. It must NEVER be mixed with
   ChatConversation, which is the AI agent talking to GARAGES' OWN CUSTOMERS. Merging them would
   put thousands of MOT bookings into the support queue.
8. Unassigned is a SHARED queue. It is not defaulted to one person.

=== BUILD ORDER (dependency, not preference) ===
Phase 2's TICKET MODEL is the foundation and must be built FIRST, before Phase 1's email ingest.
Ingest with no ticket model just piles email onto SupportConversation, which is one-thread-per-user
and cannot express "one ticket per issue". Order: Phase 0 → Phase 2 (ticket model) → Phase 1
(email in) → Phase 1b (noise filter) → Phase 3 (WhatsApp) → Phase 4 (calls).

=== THE TICKET MODEL IN ONE PARAGRAPH ===
One ticket = one issue, not one customer. Statuses: new (nobody looked), open (ours to act on),
pending (replied, waiting on the customer — drops out of the work queue), on_hold (blocked
internally), solved (done, reopens on reply), closed (archived after a few days). Every ticket has
a requester (a contact: email and/or phone, linked to a garage/business when we can identify them),
an optional assignee, a channel, a category, a priority, and a thread of entries that are either
public replies or internal staff-only notes.

=== DEFINITION OF DONE FOR THE WHOLE PROJECT ===
A garage emails hello@, WhatsApps the ops number, or escalates from live chat; each becomes a
ticket in one queue; staff see Unassigned / Mine open / Pending 3+ days; every reply reaches the
customer on the right channel; spam creates no tickets; supplier invoices route to Dan; and the
9pm ops report can state how many tickets were opened, answered and left outstanding.
