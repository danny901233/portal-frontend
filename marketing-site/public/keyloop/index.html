The hard part was never the voice

Answering the phone convincingly is close to solved, and cheaper every quarter. Eighteen months went into what sits behind it.

Diary writes

Nearly two-thirds of our sites run against a live DMS. Real availability, the garage's own price list, a confirmed booking written into the workshop diary — not a message for someone to action later.

Integrations

Five systems in production: Garage Hive, Tyresoft, Bookar, Autosage and our own. The sixth is a fortnight, not a quarter.

Vehicle capture

Registration capture over a bad line is the most failure-prone moment in a garage call. Phonetic read-back, escalation to spelling when a caller stalls, validation against DVLA data.

Out of hours

A fifth of calls arrive outside 8am–6pm. Those are the ones a garage loses entirely, and the easiest value for an owner to see in month one.

Three garages, three different wins

All live six months or more. Conversion is measured the same way throughout: of the callers who got as far as choosing a service, how many left with a confirmed booking.

80% converted

An independent workshop where four in five callers who reach service selection leave with a job in the diary — the highest on the platform. Its diary is well kept and its service list is clean, which is what the ceiling looks like when the data behind the AI is right.

Five branches

A fast-fit chain that came on as a single account and rolled out branch by branch — four live, the fifth in set-up — each with its own diary, number and opening hours. It converts at 65% and is the closest thing we have to a franchise model.

Out of hours

A performance and tuning specialist taking 23% of its calls outside 8am–6pm, converting those at 63%. Nearly one call in four would previously have reached an answerphone.

Where we're taking it

Two tracks: more garages we can reach, and more the AI can do for each one.

Networks

Beyond the DMS integrations, we sell through garage networks and parts distribution — ServiceSure, GSF and Select, alongside our existing platform partners, with more in the pipeline. One agreement puts us in front of hundreds of garages rather than one.

Outbound

Two jobs, both on the phone. Pre-appointment calls that confirm tomorrow's bookings and cut no-shows, and service and MOT reminder calls that ring a customer when the vehicle is due and book it straight back in. This is the step that turns us from a cost the garage saves into revenue it would not otherwise have had.

Territory

Little in the stack is UK-specific beyond the vehicle lookup and the accent. We are adding French now, with further languages to follow — our site already runs in it. The speech and language models underneath are multilingual as they stand, so the work sits in the garage-specific parts rather than the voice. Relevant if the territory model is the direction you take.
